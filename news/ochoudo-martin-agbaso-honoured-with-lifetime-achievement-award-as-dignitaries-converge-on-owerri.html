OCHOUDO MARTIN AGBASO HONOURED WITH LIFETIME ACHIEVEMENT AWARD AS DIGNITARIES CONVERGE ON OWERRI

OWERRI, OCTOBER 10, 2026

It was a remarkable gathering of faith, leadership, enterprise and public service as the Catholic Men's Association honoured Ochoudo Martin Agbaso with a prestigious Lifetime Achievement Award in Owerri.

The occasion attracted an impressive assembly of personalities, including former Anambra State Governor and Nigeria Democratic Party (NDC) presidential candidate, Mr Peter Obi, the Catholic Archbishop of Owerri, Most Rev. Lucius Iwejuru Ugorji, prominent political figures, a delegation of NDC candidates, respected business leaders and community stakeholders.

Among the notable business personalities present was Chief Chidi Anyaegbu (Chisco), whose attendance added to the distinguished gathering.

For much of the occasion, Owerri became a centre of attention as dignitaries, well-wishers and members of the public converged to witness the celebration.

Beyond the award itself, the occasion offered a moment to reflect on a lifetime of enterprise, public engagement and contributions to society.

For Ochoudo Martin Agbaso, whose interests span business, community development and politics, the recognition represents another chapter in a public life that has attracted both attention and acknowledgement over the years.

The gathering also demonstrated something important about public recognition: that contributions to society can be acknowledged across political, professional and religious boundaries.

The presence of personalities from different walks of life gave the occasion a significance extending beyond the presentation of an award.

October 10, 2026, will remain a memorable date in the record of this recognition.

Congratulations to Ochoudo Martin Agbaso on his Lifetime Achievement Award.
