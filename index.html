THE MOVEMENT

More Than a Political Movement.

Welcome to a movement built on hope, unity, service and the vision of a better Imo State.

The Ochoudo Mandate Group brings together supporters, admirers and believers in the leadership ideals of Ochoudo Martin Agbaso, a man whose lifelong commitment to the welfare of the people continues to inspire a new generation.

Our mission is to promote constructive engagement, share ideas, mobilise grassroots participation and champion a vision of an Imo State where good governance, quality healthcare, education, economic empowerment and social justice are accessible to all.

MEET OCHOUDO

Ochoudo Martin Agbaso, MFR

Ochoudo Martin Agbaso is a distinguished entrepreneur, political leader and public servant whose life has been defined by enterprise, courage and an enduring commitment to people.

Across decades of business and public life, he has demonstrated the capacity to build, lead and inspire. Today, a new generation is rediscovering the values that have defined his journey, courage, compassion, integrity and service.
